CLIMATE · ANTALYA

COP31 ends with no deal

Two days into overtime, the longest UN climate summit on record closed without a pact, after the EU and island states refused a text that never named fossil fuels.

COP31 President Murat Kurum, Turkey's environment minister, told the plenary there was no agreement and the decisions were not adopted. Ireland's Darragh O'Brien, speaking for the EU, called the omission “unconscionable”. Panama, Vanuatu, Belize and the Marshall Islands also withheld support. António Guterres said there may be no consensus in the room, but “no ambiguity in the science”. The file passes to COP32 in Addis Ababa.
